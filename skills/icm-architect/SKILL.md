## Why the rename

`icm-architect` named the *protocol*. The skill is Griot's *implementation* of that protocol, and
the suite now calls that implementation **Spectrum** — contract vs implementation, like POSIX and
Linux.

**ICM keeps its own name everywhere it appears.** The method is ICM (Van Clief & McDermott,
arXiv:2603.16021, MIT); `icm/` in this repo stays `icm/` because that is attribution, not
preference. Only the *skill* took the Griot name.

This alias is therefore not a countdown to deletion — it is also the attribution surface. Both
names resolve, and the old one is not going away.
